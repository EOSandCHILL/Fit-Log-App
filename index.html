FitLog Instructions

Welcome to FitLog!

Here’s how to use FitLog:

  1. Click on the map to log a new workout.
  2. Fill in the details for your workout (type, distance, etc.).
  3. Your workout will be saved and displayed on the map.
  4. You can click on any workout to see its details.

Enjoy tracking your fitness journey!
